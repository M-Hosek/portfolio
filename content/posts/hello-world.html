Your Education and the Good Life

2022-2024 · Stanford University

This is a sample syllabus based on a course I taught at Stanford University from 2022-2024. It was developed by the program for Civic, Liberal, and Global Education.

Course Description

With the a world of information at our fingertips daily, many have begun to re-evaluate the value and purpose of a college education. Is it just to train you for a career? Is it worthwhile to take a wide range of courses in topics that won't translate to a post-college job? Is it better to focus on your success in college, or your happiness? And should we think of higher education as a private commodity or a public good? These are just some of the questions that have entered contemporary American discourse.

In this course, students engage with a wide range of historical, philosophical, literary, and artistic sources that offer different perspectives on what higher education should be like. We debate whether these opinions--from Plato to Steve Jobs--are valueable resources for the 21st century college student. Students draw upon sources from around the world to develop their own theory of how to craft a meanginful higher-ed experience
