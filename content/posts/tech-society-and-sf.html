Tech, Society, and Science Fiction

Last updated: 7 August 2026 · Stanford University

Science Fiction from and about East Asian contexts are more accessible than ever in the English-speaking world. In this class, we examine the hopes, fears, and concerns regarding technology that are expressed through these science fictions. In examining how notable texts, films, and works of art make this genre mode to comment on real world issues, we will debate their relevance to today’s discussions about technological change. We will take a comparative approach to draw out the particular aesthetic and intellectual traditions of Asian cultures and also to observe where commonalities in ideas that transcend borders. Students will learn skills related to literary and aesthetic analysis, ethical reasoning, global perspectives, and communication.
