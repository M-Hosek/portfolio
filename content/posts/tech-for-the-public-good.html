Technology for the Public Good

Last updated: 19 August 2026

This syllabus was developed for Hobart and William Smith Colleges' program for Management and Entrepreneurship. It is based on two courses I taught at Stanford University: Citizenship in the 21st Century and The Ethics and Politics of Public Service.

Course Itinerary

Week 1: Your Education and The Public Good

Gerhard Casper, "The University as Public Service"

Emily Levin and Mitchell Stevens, "Negotiating the Academic Social Contract"

Week 2: Social Capital

Robert Putnam, Bowling Alone: The Collapse and Revival of American Community

Week 3: The Intended and Unintended Consequences of Technology Change

Langdon Winner, The Whale in the Reactor

Walter Benjamin, "The Telephone"

Week 4: Approaches to Governing Technology Change

Plato, "Phadreas"

NYT, "In Amish Country, the Future is Calling"

Gutmann and Moreno, "Keeping CRSPR Safe

Week 5: Civil Society and the Non-Profit Sector

Alexis de Tocqueville, Democracy in America, chapter 5

Norman Silber, A corporate Model of Freedom: The Emergence of the Modern Nonprofit Sector

Week 6: Can Private Business be a Public Service?

Milton Friedman, "The Social Responsibility of Business is to Increase its PRofits"

Andrew Carnegie, The Gospel of Wealth

Janine Hiller, "The Benefit Corporation and Corporate Social Responsibility"

Week 7: Social Media and Society

Philip Howard, Muzammil Hussain, "The Upheavals in Egypt and Tunesia: The Role of Social Media"

Marshall Ganz and Art Reyes, "Reclaiming Civil Society"

Week 8: Industry Case Study: Big Tech

Karen Hao, Empire of AI

Week 9: Is the Future of Tech All Bad?

Lee Kai-fu and Chen Qiufan, AI 2041

Week 10: Sustainability in the Supply Chain

Andrew Savitz, The Triple Bottom Line: How Today's Best Run Companies are Achieving Economic, Social, and Environmental Success

Week 11: The Social Hazzards of Automation

Walter Benjamin, "The Work of Art in the Age of Mechanical Reproduction"

dir. Yu Haibo and Yu "Kiki" Tianqi, China's Van Goghs

Hao Jingfang, Folding Beijing

Week 12: Innovating for Social Justice

Lesley-Ann Noel, Design Social Change

dir. Heesun Shin and Chihyung Jeon, Hug Me Tight: A Robot Story

Week 13: Business Model Workshop 1: Sketching the vision

With guest entrepreneur

Week 14: Business Model Workshop 2: Exploratory Data Analysis Prototype

Review of Data analytics skills and tools, including PowerBi, Excel, R, etc.

Week 15: Business Model Workshop 3: Complete and Present Your Pitch

Review communication skills and technologies (Powerpoint, Canva, etc.)
